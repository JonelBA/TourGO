Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our rental services.

How old do I need to be to rent a car?
Our standard minimum age requirement is 25 years. However, drivers aged 21-24 may rent with a young driver surcharge. Some premium and luxury vehicles require drivers to be 25+ with no exceptions.
What documents do I need to rent a car?
You'll need a valid driver's license, a major credit card in the renter's name, and a second form of ID (passport or ID card). International customers may need an International Driving Permit depending on their country of origin.
Is there a security deposit required?
Yes, a security deposit is required and will be placed as a hold on your credit card at the time of pickup. The amount varies based on the vehicle category, typically ranging from $200 for economy cars to $500+ for luxury vehicles.
Can I add additional drivers to my rental?
Yes, additional drivers can be added to your rental agreement for a daily fee. Each additional driver must meet our age requirements and present a valid driver's license at the time of rental.
