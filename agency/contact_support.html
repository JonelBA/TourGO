Agency Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our agency partnership.

How do payments work for agencies?
Payments are processed weekly every Tuesday. You'll receive payment for all completed rentals from the previous week (Monday-Sunday). Minimum payout amount is $100. Payments are made via direct deposit or PayPal.
What are the vehicle requirements?
Vehicles must be no older than 7 years, have valid registration and insurance, pass our safety inspection, and meet our cleanliness standards. Luxury and specialty vehicles may have additional requirements.
How do I handle vehicle maintenance?
Regular maintenance is your responsibility. For rentals affected by maintenance, mark the vehicle as unavailable in your dashboard. We recommend using our partner network for discounted maintenance services.
Can I set my own pricing?
Yes, you have full control over your daily, weekly, and monthly rates. We provide market rate suggestions, but you set the final prices. Minimum rates may apply in some categories.
